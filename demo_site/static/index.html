TekmerDB Demo

Upload a small PDF or paste text and TekmerDB will extract claims and score confidence mechanically — not guessed by an LLM. When two claims conflict, both are flagged, not silently resolved.

This is a single shared public demo instance — all data is wiped every hour.

Try the contradiction demo: insert "Drug X approved by FDA" from source FDA-2024, then insert "Drug X rejected by FDA due to safety concerns" from source FDA-2025-review — watch both drop in confidence and flag a conflict.
